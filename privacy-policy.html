Privacy Policy — Ethin Studio Publisher

Effective date: September 27, 2026

1. Who we are

Ethin Studio Publisher is a personal publishing tool operated by the owner of the TikTok account it serves. It is not a public service and has no other users.

2. What we collect

  • Your TikTok account ID and username, obtained through TikTok Login Kit when you authorize the app.
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok for your account.
  • Video files, captions, and hashtags that you provide for the purpose of publishing.

We do not collect your password, payment information, contacts, or any data beyond what is listed above.

3. How we use it

Your data is used solely to schedule and publish videos to your own TikTok account via the official TikTok Content Posting API, acting only on your explicit instructions. We never post content you did not provide or approve.

4. How we store it

Data is stored server-side. OAuth tokens are encrypted at rest and access is limited to the publishing function described above.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your data. We do not use your data for advertising. We do not share your data with any third party except TikTok itself, through its official API, as required to publish your videos.

6. Revoking access

You can revoke the app's authorization at any time in your TikTok app settings under authorized third-party apps. Once revoked, the app can no longer access your account, and your stored tokens are deleted. You may also request deletion of your stored videos at any time via the contact below.

7. Data retention

Video files are deleted after they are published, or earlier on your request. Tokens are deleted when you revoke authorization.

8. Children

This tool is not directed at children under 13 and is not a public service.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
